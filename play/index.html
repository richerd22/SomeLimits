your first coaster in five minutes

  • hit start building. you get a working coaster immediately - the presets are complete circuits, not demos.
  • drag any lime node to move it. the height and banking sliders shape the selected node.
  • make a hill taller than the rest - any climb of 8m or more grows a lift chain by itself.
  • double-click the track to add a node exactly there. click empty ground to drop a node where you click instead. banking past +/-90 degrees is how you build inversions: you are the loop menu.
  • shift-drag (or right-drag) a node sideways to bank it - the track twists as you pull. the slider drives the same number; both stay in sync.
  • when you let go of a drag, a gentle smoothing pass runs over the neighbourhood. that's deliberate - it's the fix for kinked connections.
  • press RIDE IT (or R). the train leaves the station, gravity does the rest, brakes bring it home.

riding

  • C cycles camera: POV, chase, orbit. ESC gets out. the HUD shows speed, height, live g.
  • at the end of the lap you get the numbers: time, max speed, max/min g, airtime.

saving + sharing

  • save to library keeps named coasters in this browser. the library shows a top-down blueprint of each one.
  • json downloads the coaster as a file - the real backup. import reads json or NL2-style csv.
  • nl2 csv exports the track spline (position + up vectors) for NoLimits 2-style importers.
  • copy share link hides the whole coaster inside the page URL. send the link, they get your coaster.

honest faq

  • is this NoLimits? no. it's a loving knockoff that owns its limits. no shared code, no shared assets.
  • where are my saves? in your browser's localStorage, nowhere else. clear your browser data and they're gone - export json for keeps.
  • can it do parks / multiple coasters / terrain? not in v1. one coaster, flat ground, full heart.
