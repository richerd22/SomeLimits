your first coaster in five minutes

  • two ways in: new coaster gives you a blank plot - click the ground to place the station, click again to lay points out from it (each point lands at ground level, so a drop is just what happens after a hill), and click the station to close the circuit. or take a preset - complete circuits, not demos, rideable the second they land.
  • drag a lime point up/down to change height, or drag it sideways to move it across the plot. the first clear direction wins for that drag. drag the orange cap above it for precise height - the slider drives the same number. no extra tools.
  • make a hill taller than the rest - any climb of 8m or more grows a lift chain by itself, drawn as a bright strip on the track. nobody presses a lift button; there effectively isn't one.
  • double-click the track to add a node exactly there. click empty ground to drop a node where you click instead. banking past +/-90 degrees rolls the train over - rolls are banking, loops are shapes (next section). you are the loop menu.
  • drag the blue grip around the selected node to bank it - the grip rides a ring around the point on the end of a bar, sitting wherever the current bank has rolled it; pull it around and the track rolls with your hand, degrees floating by the point. shift-drag (or right-drag) a node sideways does the same thing the old way, and the slider drives the same number; all three stay in sync.
  • the point stays where you drag it. the neighbours and their banking do not shift when you let go. the track between points is smoothed by the spline, not by moving your points behind your back.
  • press RIDE IT (or R). the train leaves the station, gravity does the rest, brakes bring it home.

loops and rolls (you will try these first)

  • a vertical loop is a shape, not a setting: run straight, then stack points over the same ground in a circle - up one side, across the top, down the other, out the bottom. points may share a ground spot when their heights differ; the stacking is the technique, not a mistake. the looper preset is one, ready to ride and pull apart.
  • the loop spends the drop: keep the loop's top well below your highest point. the train pays for every metre of loop out of the fall before it - a loop nearly as tall as your lift ends with the train crawling over the top at walking pace. smaller loop or taller hill: those are the only fixes, same as the real ones.
  • a corkscrew is banking: set four points in a row to 0, 120, -120, 0 degrees. the blend wraps the short way round, so after 120 the roll keeps turning the same direction through -120 instead of unwinding - one full roll, no menu involved.

riding

  • C cycles camera: POV, chase, orbit. ESC gets out. the HUD shows speed, height, live g.
  • sound is generated, not recorded: chain clack on the lift, wind that follows your speed. the HUD's sound button shuts it up.
  • at the end of the lap you get the numbers: time, max speed, max/min g, airtime.

saving + sharing

  • save to library keeps named coasters in this browser. the library shows a top-down blueprint of each one.
  • json downloads your coaster - even a half-built open draft. import restores that draft to keep building or reads a completed coaster or NL2-style csv.
  • nl2 csv exports the track spline (position + up vectors) for NoLimits 2-style importers.
  • copy share link hides the whole coaster inside the page URL. send the link, they get your coaster.

honest faq

  • is this NoLimits? no. it's a loving knockoff that owns its limits. no shared code, no shared assets.
  • where are my saves? in your browser's localStorage, nowhere else. clear your browser data and they're gone - export json for keeps.
  • can it do parks / multiple coasters / terrain editing? no. one coaster, riding on code-drawn hills you can't shape, full heart.
